Who we are

{{ site.business_name }} is a travel and transportation business based in {{ site.location }}. In this policy, "we", "us" and "BLESS" mean this business.

What we collect

When you use this website to ask for a booking or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The details of your request, such as the service you are interested in, your dates, the number of people, pick-up and drop-off places, and any notes you add.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends to any website, such as your IP address and device type.

We do not ask for card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers through this website.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your request and provide the service you asked for.
  • To contact you about your request.
  • To keep our business records.
  • To keep this website secure and prevent spam and misuse.

We use your information because you give it to us, with your consent, when you contact us or send a request.

Who we share it with

Only people who need it to handle your request can see it. We also use trusted service providers to run this website, such as providers of hosting, data storage, email delivery and spam protection. They may only use your information to provide those services to us.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We may share it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above and for our business and legal records. You can ask us to correct or delete your information (see "Your rights" below).

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No online system is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive details in the notes you send us.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It may use cookies that are needed for it to work safely. Some content, such as text fonts, is loaded from other providers, and they may see your IP address when it loads.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used.
  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Object to how we use it.
  • Ask us to delete or block it.
  • Receive a copy of it in a common format.
  • Claim damages if your information is misused.
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph (opens in a new tab).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
