Who we are

{{ site.business_name }} is a travel and transportation business based in {{ site.location }}. In this policy, "we", "us" and "BLESS" mean this business.

What we collect

When you send a booking request, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your request details: the tour or car you chose, your dates and times, the number of passengers, pick-up and drop-off places, destination, whether you want a driver, and any notes you add.
  • A 6-digit code we email you to check that the address is yours. It is only used for that and it expires in 10 minutes.
  • Our own record of your request: its reference number, its status (for example pending or approved) and its payment status.

We do not ask for card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers through this website.

Why we use it

  • To check your email address and to review and process your request.
  • To contact you by phone, Messenger or email about your request, and to email you when it is approved.
  • To keep records of bookings and payments.
  • To block spam and misuse of the booking form.

We use your information because you give it to us, with your consent, when you send a booking request.

Who can see it

BLESS staff who handle bookings can see your request. They sign in to a private admin area to do so. We also use trusted services to run the website: Google Firebase stores the booking records, an email service (Gmail or Resend) sends our emails, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks for bots when it is turned on.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We may share it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to handle your booking and for our business records. You can ask us to correct or delete your information (see "Your rights" below).

Keeping it safe

Booking records can only be opened by signed-in BLESS administrators. No online system is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive details in the notes field.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The BLESS admin area uses a sign-in cookie. Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile), when it is on, runs from Cloudflare. These services may see your IP address when they load.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used.
  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Object to how we use it.
  • Ask us to delete or block it.
  • Receive a copy of it in a common format.
  • Claim damages if your information is misused.
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph (opens in a new tab).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
