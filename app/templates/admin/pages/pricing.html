Car pricing

Extra-hour charges for car rentals. Each car's daily rate is set on its own page under Cars. Changes apply to new requests; bookings already received keep their price.

{% include 'admin/components/notice.html' %} {{ errors_box(errors) }}

How extra hours are charged

The rental is counted in 24-hour blocks from the pick-up time to the return time, and each block costs the car's daily rate. The hours left over are charged like this. Part of an hour counts as a full hour.
